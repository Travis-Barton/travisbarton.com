Privacy policy

Effective September 12, 2026. This policy covers Annie Weekly, the owner-operated agent briefing email integration.

Data and purpose

Annie Weekly uses the authorized Google account email address and send-only Gmail permission to send owner-requested briefings to owner-selected recipients. It does not request inbox-reading permission. The owner supplies recipient addresses, and agent updates supply the newsletter content.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored in the operator’s macOS Keychain. Drafts, recipient configuration and delivery records are stored in the operator’s local workspace. Newsletter messages are transmitted through Google Gmail to the designated recipients and their email providers. The public app pages do not contain private newsletters or authentication credentials.

This integration does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use Gmail access to train generalized AI models. Google user data is used only to provide the requested email functionality, consistent with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and retention

The owner can revoke access in Google Account permissions and disable the schedule at any time. Revoking access does not delete messages already sent. Local drafts, configuration and delivery records are retained until the operator removes them; requests for removal or questions can be sent to granny.assist.agent@gmail.com.
